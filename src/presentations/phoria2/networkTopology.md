# {{label}}

Renders a 3D network topology using [phoria2](https://github.com/kevinroast/phoria2), based on its topology demo. Core routers, a ring of distribution switches and their hosts are coloured by status. Hover over a node to see its details and trace its links, drag to turn the map, and click a node to show it in the summary.
